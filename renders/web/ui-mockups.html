Principles

A dedicated player, not a small phone (SYS-017). Familiar patterns are used where they help, and each one is chosen and shaped for this device, not copied.

One instrumentThe screen and the four buttons are one interface. What a button does is drawn beside it: volume appears next to the volume keys, power next to Main (natural mapping).
Music is the homeThe T1 wakes where it left off, in Now Playing. The library lies below it and the queue to its right: one spatial model, no tab bar (T1-051, SYS-039).
Content, not chromeType, artwork and hairlines carry the structure. No cards inside cards, no floating buttons, no hamburger; 16 px margins, so the 3.5" panel is used for content.
Engineered clarityTechnical values in Plex Mono, aligned like an instrument's readout; the sound drawn as its signal chain, the way an engineer draws it (design system §1, SYS-041).
Eyes-free firstWhat matters while walking is on a button; the screen confirms it. Primary actions sit in the lower half, and nothing important depends on a gesture alone (E14).
Proximity, deliberatelySpacing says what belongs together: 0–4 px inside a unit (title and artist), 8–12 px inside a group, 16–24 px between groups. Never one gap for everything.
