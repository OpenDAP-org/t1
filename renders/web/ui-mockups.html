T1 interface mockups

The T1's interface at the panel's real size (320 × 480, one CSS pixel per panel pixel), in the Engineered Matter tokens, from the ecosystem experience research, the controls and gestures research, the audio profiles spec and the design system. Draft, 2026-10-23, for reference: layouts are to be confirmed on the virtual T1. Library content is illustrative; artwork is a placeholder. Devices and sync screens: devices-and-sync.html.

Navigation

A dedicated player, not a phone (SYS-017): no launcher and no app grid. Music opens on the library, the player is one tap away from anywhere through the mini player, and every edge gesture has a visible control (SYS-039, WCAG 2.5.1). Depth stays at three levels or fewer from the library to any track.
