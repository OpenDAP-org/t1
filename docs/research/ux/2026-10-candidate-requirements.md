# UX research: candidate functional requirements for the T1

- **Date:** 2026-10-22
- **Status:** Consolidation of desk research. Nothing here is a decision and no requirements table has been changed. Every row is a **candidate** for the maintainers to accept, reword or drop; accepted rows move to the owning repository's requirements file with that repository's own ID.
- **Sources consolidated:**
  - [Personas, jobs to be done and journey][jtbd] (cited as **JTBD**);
  - [Playback, signal path, library, connectivity and device behaviour][plo] (cited as **PLO**, with its local IDs GP, SP, LB, CN, DV);
  - [Modding and custom-firmware communities][mod] (cited as **MOD**, with its §8 row numbers and §9 risks).
- **Checked against, to avoid duplicates:** [T1][req-t1] (T1-…), [system][req-sys] (SYS-…), [Blackbird OS][req-os] (OS-…), [board][req-brd] (BRD-…), [UI components][req-ds] (DS-…), [desktop][req-desk] (DESK-…), [toolkit CLI][req-tk-cli] and [toolkit verify][req-tk-verify] (TK-…), [touch faceplate][req-fpt] (FPT-…), the [controls and status LED spec][controls], and [Blackbird OS ADR 0006][adr-os-0006] (on-board PCM to 192 kHz, native DSD to DSD256, DoP to DSD64, DSD512 converted and labelled, native over USB).

## How to read this document

- **Rules applied.** The T1 is a DAP first (T1-040); software uses 100 % of the hardware and the only limits are safety limits that are documented and visible (T1-045); every claim needs evidence (T1-026). Candidates that would choose a software stack or open a topic the maintainers have not opened are not written as requirements; they appear under [Decisions for the maintainers](#decisions-for-the-maintainers).
- **Wording.** "The T1 shall …" for product behaviour that spans hardware and software, "Blackbird OS shall …" for the image, engine and services, "OpenDAP Desktop shall …" for the host application.
- **Priority** is a proposal: **must** where the item is part of the audiophile experience (T1-040), a direct application of T1-045 or T1-026, or a recurring failure in the evidence; **should** otherwise. "should (D-n)" means the row depends on decision D-n.
- **Owner** is the repository where the requirement would live if accepted.
- **Relation:** **new**, or **refines X** when an existing requirement states the goal and the candidate makes it specific and testable. Items already fully covered are not in the tables; they are listed in [Appendix A](#appendix-a-already-covered).
- **Merges.** Where the three studies say the same thing, the row keeps the strongest wording and cites every origin. Numeric targets the studies did not set are left to the maintainers (D-6); none is invented here.

## Decisions for the maintainers

The open questions raised by the three studies that the maintainers have not yet settled, with the options and a recommendation consistent with the workspace rules. Questions settled on 2026-10-22 (themes, sideloading, permissions, hearing-safety scope, warranty) are in [Appendix A](#appendix-a-already-covered).

| # | Question | Options | Recommendation | Affects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D-1 | **Alternative product shells** (architecture proposal: no third-party activities) | (a) a supported image variant, a build option of OS-010; (b) a documented but unsupported path; (c) out of scope. | **(b)** now, with UX-072 guaranteeing the way back to the official image; reconsider (a) once the shell API is stable. Keeps SYS-017 for the official product without closing the device. | UX-068, UX-072 |
| D-2 | **Future verified-boot enforcement** (ADR 0005) | (a) never enforce; (b) enforce only with owner key enrolment and a disable path; (c) enforce with OpenDAP keys only. | **(b)**, recorded before any lock is considered; (c) contradicts T1-045. | UX-066 |
| D-3 | **Plug detection on both jacks** | (a) the board provides a detect signal from the 3.5 mm and the 4.4 mm jack, read by the SoC; (b) no detection. | **(a)**, as a board requirement. Without it UX-011 (pause on unplug, no music to the speaker by surprise) cannot be met, and that is part of the audiophile experience. | UX-011, board |
| D-4 | **Low-gain mode for sensitive IEMs** (ADR 0012 fixes amplifier gain at about 1.6) | (a) keep fixed gain and attenuate in the DAC; (b) add a selectable analog low-gain step. | **Decide from measurement:** measure noise at the lowest volume into a sensitive-IEM load (UX-020) on the first prototype; if residual noise is audible, (b) is required by T1-040. | UX-017, UX-020, UX-022 |
| D-5 | **Licensed protocols:** AirPlay receiver, Roon Ready, Spotify Connect, Tidal and Qobuz Connect, LDAC sink | (a) pursue each certification or licence; (b) none in the official image; (c) leave them to third-party capabilities. | **(b) by default**, with each programme evaluated one by one on cost and terms. Evidence: shairport-sync is uncertified and limited to 24-bit/48 kHz; Roon Ready has no public SDK; Spotify Connect hardware needs a partner agreement and new applications are currently limited; librespot is unofficial; an LDAC sink needs a Sony licence; Tidal and Qobuz Connect are **UNVERIFIED**. Unofficial implementations of licensed protocols should not ship in the official image. | UX-046, UX-047 |
| D-6 | **Numeric targets:** library size, control latency, boot-to-music time (and the output-impedance target of UX-020) | Set now, or after first measurements. | **Library:** set now, since it is testable on the virtual T1 (OS-018); PLO proposes at least 200,000 tracks (HiBy's flag raises 80,000 to 200,000; Sony documents 20,000). **Control latency and boot-to-music:** measure first on the virtual T1, then set targets on the prototype; no current-DAP figures were found. **Output impedance:** from measurement, against the 1/8 rule. | UX-013, UX-029, UX-055, UX-020 |
| D-7 | **Streaming and network renderer scope** | (a) local playback only for the first release; (b) open protocols: UPnP/DLNA and OpenHome renderer, SMB and NFS shares; (c) streaming services. | **Open the topic explicitly first** (the workspace rule is not to get ahead). If opened, start with (b): open standards, no licence, standard Linux software; (c) depends on D-5. | UX-047, UX-048 |
| D-8 | **MQA** | (a) play as the PCM it contains; (b) decode. | **(a).** MQA Ltd went into administration in 2023 and Tidal replaced MQA with FLAC in 2024; decoding would need a licence for a declining format. | UX-028 |
| D-9 | **File transfer mechanism** | USB mass storage (the device cannot use the exported storage meanwhile), MTP (no native macOS support, timestamps lost), a network share, transfer through OpenDAP Desktop, or a combination. | Choose in blackbird-os against UX-075. Any choice must work on macOS without a vendor tool and let the T1 keep playing from the other storage. | UX-075 |
| D-10 | **Booting an image from SD/TF without touching internal storage** | Feasibility question, not a policy one. | Verify RK3566 boot order and the A/B scheme before accepting UX-069. | UX-069 |
