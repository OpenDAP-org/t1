# Concept art

These images are screenshots of interactive 3D concept models from earlier experiments (artifacts dated 2026-10-22). They are kept here as visual inspiration only.

**They are not designs, specifications or decisions.** The captures give the general visual idea, but their parts and exploded views often have glitches and bad design decisions, because they were not based on real data or simulations. Dimensions, part choices, component placement and layouts shown in them may be wrong and must not be taken from them. This also applies to any text visible in the captures (title panels, labels, numbers): it comes from the old experiment and is not confirmed information.

The real design is built in this repository, with simulation and real structure.

## Images

- `t1-three-quarter.png`: Three-quarter front view of the assembled handheld: dark graphite rounded body, large screen showing a music player mock-up, ribbed side key.
- `t1-back.png`: Straight-on back view: plain dark back cover with an embossed logo and an orange key on the right edge.
- `t1-exploded.png`: Exploded view: screen front, circuit board, body frame, a pale battery-like block and back cover separated along the depth axis.
